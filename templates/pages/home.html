Find and play any web radio.

Know what’s playing, keep track of what you heard. Soundfolk is a free desktop app for Linux, Windows and macOS, with the tens of thousands of stations of the Radio-Browser catalog. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Every station, one search away

Type a name, a city or a genre: Soundfolk searches the open Radio-Browser catalog, from big broadcasters to one-person web radios. Stations that work come first; the ones whose last check failed stay visible, with a warning.

Not sure what to play? Surprise me picks a music station at random from the whole world. Your own streams (a direct address, .m3u or .pls) sit next to the catalog’s, and stay on your computer.

$shot_discover

Know what’s on air

The artist and title show as soon as the stream announces them. Many stations don’t: Soundfolk then reads them from the station’s website, with recipes written by listeners, or from its streaming server’s status page.

Sound starts in about half a second, the buffer rides out network hiccups, and Constant volume keeps the same loudness from one station to the next, so you never reach for the volume after switching.

$shot_sheet

Never lose a discovery

Every track you hear is written down, by day, with its station. Like the ones you love with ♡, find them later by artist, title or station, and open any of them on YouTube, Spotify, Deezer, Apple Music, Bandcamp or Discogs in one click.

Stats rank your stations by likes per hour of listening and, if you allow it, show the artists, genres and decades of the tracks you liked.

$shot_history

And the small things

  • Mini player

    A small window that stays out of the way, with the title on air and the controls.

  • On your desktop

    Media keys, the tray icon, track notifications, launch at login, keyboard shortcuts.

  • Share a station

    Send a link with the track on air. Your friend listens in the page, or opens it in Soundfolk.

  • Lists by listeners

    Stations picked by people around a theme, in Discover and below.

  • Stats

    Likes per hour of listening, listening time by hour and day, stations’ repetition.

  • Light and dark

    Follows your system, or the theme you choose.

Yours, and only yours

Your favorites, history, likes and stats live in a database on your computer. Soundfolk has no account and no server of its own, and sends nothing about you without a switch in its settings.

What Soundfolk sends, and to whom

Free software, made with listeners

The code is open, under the MPL-2.0. Listeners teach Soundfolk to read titles from station websites, add donation links and put together lists, without writing code.
